Moi-même began as an off-beat experiment of combining contrasting musical genres. During years of playing, a unique sound arose, forming a powerful whole without the members’ diverse musical roots being lost. The musicians respect and embrace each other’s differences. Over time, this collaboration grew into a close friendship. The band writes mostly about self-experienced insecurities and emotions, because let’s face it: life can be weird, unpredictable and sometimes a bit frightening. By writing songs about aspects of life that give us chills, Moi-même normalizes opening up about such emotions. In this manner, the band serves as an emotional outlet, not just for themselves but also for their listeners.

Merchandise

The Moi-même merch collection consists of second-hand fabrics in its entirety and is decorated in collaboration with Roos van de Klundert (check out her work at @fotos.van.roos). This way, our items are crafted in an environmentally friendly way and each piece is unique.

 

All items are available for purchase at our shows. If you're interested in purchasing any items online, please contact us! Send a message to moi.meme.band.official to discuss your wishes and we’ll work together to find the best fit for you
